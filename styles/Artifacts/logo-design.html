Zeros — logo explorations

The app's loaders now speak one language: a 4 × 4 grid of flush square tiles. The agent Puzzle keeps its corners and slides tiles through six holes, Orbit laps the ring, Stream runs its rows, and the transcript's square runs in soft glass. These are options for a mark and wordmark in that language.

The Puzzle Z is literally one of the agent puzzle's own squares: ten tiles, six holes, every corner filled. When the agent works, it's the logo that shuffles. On a four-by-four grid its diagonal is only two steps, so small it can read as 工; the Tall, Slim and Sheared Zs and the tiled versions read as a Z at every size. Each card shows a mark on the dark and light surfaces at 112px, then 48, 32, 24 and 16px. Copy SVG copies the mark.
