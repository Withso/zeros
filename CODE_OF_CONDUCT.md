# Code of Conduct

Zeros is a public technical project. Participation in repository discussions,
security coordination, and other project spaces must remain professional,
constructive, and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Discuss ideas and evidence, not a person's identity or motives.
- Be clear, patient, and respectful across differences in experience and
  communication style.
- Keep reports relevant and protect private, personal, and security-sensitive
  information.
- Accept moderation decisions and stop an interaction when asked.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized content, sustained disruption,
impersonation, spam, doxxing, and publishing another person's private or
security-sensitive information are not permitted.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide, lock, or remove content and may temporarily or
permanently restrict participation when behavior harms the project or its
participants. Enforcement decisions consider context, severity, repetition,
and risk to others.

For conduct involving public GitHub content, use GitHub's **Report content**
control and the official project contact described in [SUPPORT.md](SUPPORT.md).
Security vulnerabilities must use the private process in
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not a conduct or public report. Reports will be
handled as privately as the chosen channel permits; retaliation against a
good-faith reporter is not acceptable.
