# Bind admission to the facts a consumer relies on

Runtime connect issued capabilities before provider I/O without returning or
rechecking the active boot/writer. Device-stream resume reused parked membership,
and disabling one organization's stream left mixed-organization connections open.
Neither a successful ticket nor account-level eligibility proves per-owner coverage.

Read the exact active binding with issuance, preserve its authority epoch, and
recheck it after I/O under workspace → engine → writer locks. Advertise bounded
per-organization coverage only from current flags and fresh access reads; scope
in-flight invalidation to affected readers and drain connections whose advertised
coverage changes. Revalidate a pending handshake's session before ready as well.
Fence a resume snapshot against updates learned by its connected predecessor
during the read. Missing proof preserves fallback and confirmed exact-key documents.

Encoded in S3 1.3, the runtime-connect PostgreSQL races, device-stream handshake,
mixed-organization, resume and drain tests, and standalone contract parity with
mixed-version fixtures. Endpoint-failure recovery can absorb fixture SQL errors:
race tests also assert that the intended mutation completed before checking the
refusal. Boot identity is immutable; retire the recorded engine to test boot loss.
Waiting for the writer lock can cross a lease deadline without changing its
identity; recheck wall-clock authority after the last lock and asynchronous read.
Index invalidation by the advertised organization set as well as retained
workspace rows: bounded catalogs and empty owners can advertise coverage too.
