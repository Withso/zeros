Choices to make

  • Button corners: 6px (Button, Select, Input) or 4px (IconButton, Pill, PR actions).
  • Input: keep 14px text and 12px padding, or match Button and Select at 13px and 6px.
  • Tab corners: 6px in the Tabs primitive, 8px in workbench and chat tabs.
  • Hover fill for neutral controls: --bg2-highlight or --bg2-hover.
  • Disabled: 50% everywhere, or keep 55% (Checkbox) and 70% (Label).
  • Pills: one height and one border tier for inline pills instead of eight heights.
  • Floating surfaces: 16px --bg3 menus, 8px --bg2 hover cards, 6px glass tooltips, 8px borderless toasts.
  • Chosen value in lists: check on the right (Select) or the left (Dropdown menu).
  • Focus: the --highlighted-bright ring everywhere, including Tile and inline icon buttons.
