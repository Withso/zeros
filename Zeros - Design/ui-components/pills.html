Notes

  • Eight heights (17, 18, 20, 22, 24, 26, 27 and 28px) and two radii (4 and 6px) for one idea.
  • Borders use every tier: none, --border1, --border2, --border3 and --border4.
  • Fills: none, --bg1, --bg1-hover, --bg2, --bg2-hover, --bg2-hover at 50% and --blue-pill-bg.
  • The attachment chips hover with --highlighted-bright, which is reserved for keyboard focus.
